\section{Series nomenclature}

Models are indexed by a letter-and-number code that records the experimental stage at which they were run. The \textbf{T series} denotes the final matched-benchmark stage: models trained on the relaxed-clean equal-atom MP--HZW matched dataset that underlies all main-text results. The \textbf{E series} denotes the exploratory HZW stage on an earlier data snapshot (Sections~\ref{tab:early-hzw} and~\ref{tab:e-lineage}); E-series numbers document design lineage only and are not directly comparable with the T series. The \textbf{S series} denotes the strict-filter stage: models trained on the strictly label-consistent subset (\(\mathrm{MP\ is\_metal}=\mathrm{HZW\ is\_metal}\), \(|\Delta\mathrm{gap}|\le 0.5\) eV, and per-atom volume difference \(\le 8\%\); Section~\ref{tab:size-buckets}). Within each series the numeric index is an internal run identifier; throughout the main text these codes are replaced by descriptive names. The mapping for the three headline T-series models is given in Table~\ref{tab:series-map}. This section responds to reviewer comment~7.

Training is on the relaxed lens throughout, so this table measures only the effect of the evaluation lens; it is complementary to a strict \emph{training} lens (which requires a GPU re-run). On MP, both architectural gains are masked by label noise: the virtual-node gain T4\(-\)T2 is \(-0.0081\) eV on the clean subset but \(+0.0015\) eV on the noisy complement, and the Huber gain T7\(-\)T4 is \(-0.0089\) eV (clean) but \(+0.0140\) eV (noisy). On HZW the direction differs and neither gain shrinks on the noisy complement: T4\(-\)T2 is \(-0.0101\) eV (clean) and \(-0.0141\) eV (noisy), and T7\(-\)T4 is \(-0.0071\) eV (clean) and \(-0.0186\) eV (noisy). As the complementary strict \emph{training}-lens check, we retrained the virtual-node + Huber model directly on the strict-clean MP-label dataset (S14; identical architecture and training protocol to T7, and the Huber counterpart of the MSE-loss S4 run that already exists in the S series). S14 reaches a test MAE of 0.1936 eV (RMSE 0.4308, metal F1 0.9010, \(n=8{,}154\)), versus 0.2001 eV for S4. This single strict-lens run (one seed, like its S4 counterpart) reproduces a Huber gain (\(-0.0064\) eV) of the same size as the relaxed-lens T7\(-\)T4 gain on MP (\(-0.0076\) eV): the improvement survives when the aggressive-filtering noise channel is removed by construction, which argues against---though, being a single run, cannot definitively exclude---the possibility that the Huber gain merely reflects absorption of cross-source label noise specific to the relaxed dataset.
